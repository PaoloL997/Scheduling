# Reschedule proposal test fails because of fixed 2026 dates

ui/tests/test_overlap_reassign.py `RescheduleProposalTest.setUp` and `test_compute_follows_the_current_plan`: the activities use literal dates (`self.late` from `date(2026, 8, 10)` to `date(2026, 8, 19)`, `self.blocked` from `date(2026, 8, 20)` to `date(2026, 8, 29)`) and the test moves the blocker's end to `date(2026, 10, 15)` to push the second proposal later. The reschedule horizon starts tomorrow, so once 15 October 2026 is in the past both proposals start tomorrow and `assertNotEqual(first["blocked"]["start"], second["blocked"]["start"])` fails (`'2026-11-20' == '2026-11-20'` on 19 November 2026), on main as on every branch. Anchor the dates on `localdate()` with offsets that keep the same scenario (blocker started in the past and still open, the moved `data_fine` well after tomorrow, e.g. `localdate() + timedelta(days=30)`), as the delivery date in the same `setUp` already does.
