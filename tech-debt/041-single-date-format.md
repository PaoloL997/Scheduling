# Dates are shown in five formats; use dd/mm/yyyy everywhere

Date display in the templates: the same planned dates and deadlines are shown in five formats. ui/templates/ui/pianificazione.html prints raw ISO ("2026-06-01") in the activity rows, the commessa header and the wizard recap, and dd/mm/yyyy through `_fmtIsoDay` in the notes; ui/templates/ui/index.html `kbDM` / `kbDMY` print "01/06" and "01/06/2026" in the kanban; ui/static/ui/gantt.js `fmtDate` prints "01 giu 2026" in the tooltip; ui/templates/ui/ottimizzazione_commesse.html `fmt` prints "1 giu 2026". The format to use everywhere is dd/mm/yyyy ("01/06/2026"). Add one `fmtDay(iso)` to ui/static/ui/common.js and use it in the four templates in place of the local formatters and of the raw ISO text (date inputs keep their ISO values; the Gantt axis labels are not dates of an activity and stay as they are). gantt.js is evaluated on its own by the fixtures in ui/tests/fixtures, so change `fmtDate` there to the same format instead of importing common.js. Delete `parseIsoNoon` in gantt.js, which nothing calls.
