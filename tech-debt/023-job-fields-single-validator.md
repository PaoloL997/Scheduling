# Cost, penalty and delivery date of a commessa are validated three different ways

ui/views.py `api_jobs_create` and `api_jobs_update`, ui/views_attivita.py `api_salva_attivita` (`new_job` branch): the three places that write `Job.total_cost`, `penalty` and `delivery_date` validate them differently. The jobs endpoints use `parse_money` / `parse_percent` and refuse a penalty above `penale_max`, but store `delivery_date` raw; the wizard normalises the date with `parse_day_any_format` but parses cost and penalty with `parse_measure` and has no maximum. A delivery date sent as "31/12/2026" to `/api/jobs/update/` is saved and then silently dropped by `parse_iso_day` in ui/services/planning_read.py and by `_due_date` in ui/services/optimization_problem.py, and a commessa created from the wizard can exceed the maximum penalty. Add one function in ui/services/parsing.py that validates the three fields (ISO date or 400 "Data di consegna non valida.", `parse_money`, `parse_percent`, `penale_max`) and call it from the three places.
