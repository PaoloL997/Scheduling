# Renaming or deleting an inventory item ignores its bookings

ui/services/inventory.py `patch_resource`, `update_resource` and `delete_resource`: activities reference inventory items by text (`RisorsaAttivita.risorsa_id`, no foreign key), and none of the three functions touches those rows. After a uid rename the activities keep the old uid and the renamed item looks free; after a delete they stay assigned to an item that no longer exists. On rename, update `RisorsaAttivita` rows of the matching `tipo` from the old uid to the new one in the same transaction. On delete, refuse with a 409 that lists the activities when the item is assigned to any activity without `actual_finish`; closed activities keep the uid as history. Add tests for both in ui/tests/test_inventory_api.py.
